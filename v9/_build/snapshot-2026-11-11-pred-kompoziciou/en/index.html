01 Consultation

The first joint is a Conversation

The joint holds the whole structure together. For a playground, that joint is the first conversation. You tell us who the playground is for, how many children will use it and what it should bring to your business. Everything else follows from that.

02 Site survey

A frame that fits yoUr walls

We measure the space on site: dimensions, ceiling height, entrances, windows and columns. The structure of steel tubes and joints adapts to the room, not the other way round.

03 Design and 3D visualisation

You choosE the elements

You receive a design and a 3D visualisation. You can see where the slide, ball pit, tunnel or trampoline will be. We adjust the design with you until the elements, colours and budget all fit.

04 Manufacture

Safety is in the detAils

We wrap the structure in foam and vinyl and enclose the space with nets. The playground meets EN 1176 and EN 1177. We keep tubes, joints, nets and foam in our own stock.

05 Delivery and installation

Turnkey installation

We deliver the playground, build it and hand it over ready for operation. We look after it afterwards too: service and annual inspections. Send us the dimensions of your space and what you expect from the playground. We will get back to you with a proposal.

Questions and answers

Frequent Questions

How much does an indoor children's playground cost?

The price depends on the size of the space, the number of play elements and the materials, so we cannot give it without a brief. We prepare a custom quote for every project. Send us the dimensions of your space and what you expect from the playground, and we will reply with a proposal and a price.

How long do manufacture and installation take?

The time depends on the size and complexity of the playground. We state the exact date in the quote after surveying the space. The process is always the same: consultation, survey, design and 3D visualisation, manufacture, delivery and installation.

Do you build playgrounds abroad too?

Yes, we carry out projects outside Slovakia as well. We have built playgrounds in the Czech Republic, Estonia, Romania, France and Italy. If you are planning a playground in another country, write to us and we will agree on the procedure.

What kinds of venues is an indoor playground suitable for?

It suits family centres, cafés, pastry shops, hotels, guesthouses, nurseries, education centres, shopping centres and water parks. Parents can relax while the children play, and the venue gives visitors a reason to stay longer.
