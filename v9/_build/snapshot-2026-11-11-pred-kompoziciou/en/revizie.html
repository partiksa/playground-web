03 Questions and answers

Frequent Questions

Do you carry out playground inspections?

Yes, we provide service and annual inspections of indoor playgrounds. We assess playgrounds according to EN 1176 and EN 1177, repair what is needed and replace worn parts. We keep structural tubes, joints, nets and foam in our own stock.

How often should a playground be inspected?

EN 1176-7 recommends routine visual checks by the operator, an operational inspection every one to three months and a main inspection once a year. We carry out the annual main inspection and service.
