02 Rebuild

From a Chinese import to certified

We rebuild an old playground (most often a Chinese one) into a new, certified one. We reuse material from the original: steel from the structure, joints and undamaged modules.

  • replacement of worn play elements
  • layout changes and better use of space
  • new design and colours
  • addition of new attractions
  • adjustment to current standards

03 Relocation

We dismantle, transport and rebuild

We dismantle the playground, transport it and rebuild it in a new space. We can also resize it along the way.

04 When it pays off

A rebuild makes Sense when

  1. 01

    The structure is sound

    The steel, connectors and modules are undamaged. Only the padding, nets or elements are worn.

  2. 02

    The playground does not meet the standards

    We bring an old playground, most often a Chinese one, up to the current EN 1176 and EN 1177 standards.

  3. 03

    You want a new look

    New colours, design, better use of space and new attractions without buying a whole new playground.

  4. 04

    You are moving your venue

    We dismantle, transport and rebuild the playground in the new space, adapted to the new dimensions if needed.

07 Questions and answers

Frequent Questions

Can an old playground be rebuilt instead of buying a new one?

Yes, we can rebuild an old playground, most often a Chinese one, into a new, certified one. We reuse steel from the original structure, joints and undamaged modules, so you save money. Replacing elements, changing the layout, a new design and added attractions are agreed according to the condition of the playground.

Can a playground be relocated to a new space?

Yes, we dismantle the playground, transport it and rebuild it in the new space. We can resize it or extend it along the way. The price depends on the size of the playground and the distance, and we work it out from your enquiry.
