01 Structure

Steel, foam and nEts

The load-bearing structure consists of steel tubes and joints. We wrap it in foam and vinyl, and nets enclose the space. Play elements attach to the structure: slides, tunnels, nets and hanging modules.

The playgrounds meet EN 1176 (playground equipment) and EN 1177 (impact-absorbing surfaces).

07 Questions and answers

Frequent Questions

How much space do I need for a children's playground?

We can adapt a playground to almost any space, so we do not set a minimum area. We design the structure according to the dimensions, ceiling height, entrances, windows and columns, which we measure on site. We also advise on the layout of the playground and seating for parents.

Do your playgrounds meet safety standards?

Yes, our indoor playgrounds meet EN 1176 (playground equipment) and EN 1177 (impact-absorbing surfaces). We wrap the structure in foam and vinyl and enclose the space with nets. We make playgrounds from quality materials with a focus on safety and long service life.

Will you prepare a floor plan and visualisation before manufacture?

Yes, after surveying the space you receive a design and a 3D visualisation. It shows where the slide, ball pit, tunnel or trampoline will be. We adjust the design with you until the elements, colours and budget all fit.

Do you also offer inflatable attractions?

Yes, we offer inflatable attractions as an addition to playgrounds. Available types and the price depend on your enquiry. Tell us what space and which age group you need them for.
